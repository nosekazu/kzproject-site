Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains how KZ-Project Uploader (the "Tool") handles information.

1. Information we use

The Tool connects only to KZ-Project's own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit. It uses the access token and basic account information (such as the account name) that TikTok provides, only to upload KZ-Project's own videos as drafts.

2. Information we do not collect

The Tool does not collect, store, or share any information about other TikTok users or viewers.

3. Storage

The access token is stored only on KZ-Project's own computer. It is not sent to any third party.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or share any information with third parties.

5. Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the TikTok app settings (Security / Manage app permissions).

6. Changes

This policy may be updated on this page at any time.

7. Contact

Contact KZ-Project on X: @kz_project4u

プライバシーポリシー（日本語）
